# How Does Quantization Affect Multilingual LLMs?

- **Authors:** Marchisio, Dash, Chen, Aumiller, Üstün, Hooker, Ruder
- **Venue:** Findings of EMNLP 2024
- **ID:** arXiv:2407.03211
- **BibTeX key:** `marchisio2024quantization`

## Summary

Quantization hurts non-Latin-script and lower-resource languages more; automatic metrics underestimate the damage that human raters see.

## Why we cite it

Motivation: uneven, language-dependent damage (cf. our Icelandic result at 3 bits).
