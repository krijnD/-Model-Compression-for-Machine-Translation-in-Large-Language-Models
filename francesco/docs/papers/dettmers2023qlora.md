# QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs

- **Authors:** Dettmers, Pagnoni, Holtzman, Zettlemoyer
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023
- **ID:** arXiv:2305.14314
- **BibTeX key:** `dettmers2023qlora`

## Summary

Trains LoRA adapters on top of a frozen 4-bit (NF4) base model.

## Why we cite it

Precedent for training a LoRA on a frozen quantized model, as we do.
