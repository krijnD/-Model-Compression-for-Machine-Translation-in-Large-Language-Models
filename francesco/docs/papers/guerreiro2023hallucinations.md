# Looking for a Needle in a Haystack: A Comprehensive Study of Hallucinations in Neural Machine Translation

- **Authors:** Guerreiro, Voita, Martins
- **Venue:** EACL 2023
- **ID:** arXiv:2208.05309
- **BibTeX key:** `guerreiro2023hallucinations`

## Summary

Taxonomy and detection of NMT hallucinations, including oscillatory (repetitive, over-long) outputs.

## Why we cite it

Motivates our over-long-output (>= 2x reference length) hallucination proxy.
