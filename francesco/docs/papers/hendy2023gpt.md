# How Good Are GPT Models at Machine Translation? A Comprehensive Evaluation

- **Authors:** Hendy et al.
- **Venue:** arXiv 2023
- **ID:** arXiv:2302.09210
- **BibTeX key:** `hendy2023gpt`

## Summary

Evaluates GPT models on 18 translation directions. They are competitive for high-resource languages and weaker for low-resource ones.

## Why we cite it

Evidence that decoder-only LLMs are competitive translators.
