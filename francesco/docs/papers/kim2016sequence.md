# Sequence-Level Knowledge Distillation

- **Authors:** Kim, Rush
- **Venue:** EMNLP 2016
- **ID:** arXiv:1606.07947
- **BibTeX key:** `kim2016sequence`

## Summary

Word-level and sequence-level KD for NMT; compresses large NMT models into smaller ones.

## Why we cite it

Standard reference for KD in MT; we use the word(token)-level variant.
