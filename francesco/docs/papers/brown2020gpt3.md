# Language Models are Few-Shot Learners

- **Authors:** Brown et al.
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2020
- **ID:** arXiv:2005.14165
- **BibTeX key:** `brown2020gpt3`

## Summary

GPT-3, a 175B decoder-only LM; shows in-context few-shot ability on many tasks, translation included.

## Why we cite it

The rise of decoder-only LLMs.
