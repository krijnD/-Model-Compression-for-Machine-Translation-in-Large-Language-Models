# On Information and Sufficiency

- **Authors:** Kullback, Leibler
- **Venue:** The Annals of Mathematical Statistics 22(1), 1951
- **ID:** doi:10.1214/aoms/1177729694
- **BibTeX key:** `kullback1951information`

## Summary

Defines the Kullback-Leibler divergence, an asymmetric measure of how much one probability distribution differs from another.

## Why we cite it

Our distillation loss is the forward KL from the fp16 teacher's next-token distribution to the quantized student's.
