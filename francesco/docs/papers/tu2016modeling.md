# Modeling Coverage for Neural Machine Translation

- **Authors:** Tu, Lu, Liu, Liu, Li
- **Venue:** ACL 2016
- **ID:** arXiv:1601.04811
- **BibTeX key:** `tu2016modeling`

## Summary

Defines over-translation (source content translated repeatedly) and under-translation (source content left untranslated) and adds a coverage vector to attention to reduce both.

## Why we cite it

Names for our 'under-translation' (truncated output) category, and 'over-translation' if the length-ratio flag is kept.
