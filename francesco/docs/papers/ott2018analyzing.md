# Analyzing Uncertainty in Neural Machine Translation

- **Authors:** Ott, Auli, Grangier, Ranzato
- **Venue:** ICML 2018
- **ID:** arXiv:1803.00047
- **BibTeX key:** `ott2018analyzing`

## Summary

Shows that NMT models sometimes copy the source sentence instead of translating it, and links this to noise in training data and to beam search.

## Why we cite it

Name for our 'source copy' failure category.
