# No Language Left Behind: Scaling Human-Centered Machine Translation

- **Authors:** NLLB Team (Costa-jussà et al.)
- **Venue:** arXiv 2022 (later Nature 2024)
- **ID:** arXiv:2207.04672
- **BibTeX key:** `nllb2022`

## Summary

Encoder-decoder (sparse mixture-of-experts Transformer) translation model for 200 languages; also releases FLORES-200.

## Why we cite it

Example of a large, strong, traditional encoder-decoder MT system.
