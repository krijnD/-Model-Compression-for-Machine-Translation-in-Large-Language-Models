# A Paradigm Shift in Machine Translation: Boosting Translation Performance of Large Language Models

- **Authors:** Xu, Kim, Sharaf, Hassan Awadalla
- **Venue:** ICLR 2024
- **ID:** arXiv:2309.11674
- **BibTeX key:** `xu2024alma`

## Summary

ALMA: two-stage recipe on LLaMA-2. Stage 1 is continued pre-training on monolingual data of the target languages. Stage 2 is fine-tuning on a small set of high-quality parallel data (full weights or LoRA). Languages: English with cs, de, is, zh, ru (10 directions); WMT'22 test sets, WMT'21 for Icelandic.

## Why we cite it

The model family we compress; source of the 10 directions, the training data and the prompt format.
